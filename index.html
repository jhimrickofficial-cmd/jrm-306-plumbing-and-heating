08 — Before You Call

Straight answers.

How much will my plumbing or heating repair cost? expand_more
We operate on an upfront, transparent flat-rate menu. Before our journeyperson performs any work, they evaluate your specific issue and present you with a fixed written price. There are never surprise fees or open-ended hourly billing.
Can’t I just fix this myself? expand_more
While simple tasks like toilet flappers are DIY-friendly, plumbing rough-ins, natural gas connections, boiler combustion diagnostics, and whole-home repiping must comply with strict Saskatchewan safety and building codes to protect your insurance policy and home warranty.
How fast can you arrive in an emergency? expand_more
Our average response window across Martensville, Warman, and North Saskatoon is 45 minutes for burst lines, major furnace lockouts in winter, or sewer backups. We provide live phone updates and GPS tracking en route.
What kind of warranty do I get on parts and labor? expand_more
All labor is covered by our 1-year 306 Workmanship Guarantee: if anything we repaired fails within 365 days, we return and rectify it free of charge. Most installed equipment (Bradford White tanks, Navien boilers) features manufacturer warranties of 6 to 15 years.
Do you service high-efficiency furnaces and boilers? expand_more
Yes. Our technicians hold active Saskatchewan gas fitter endorsements and are experienced with modulating boilers, in-floor hydronic heating, high-efficiency condensing furnaces, and commercial combi-units.
